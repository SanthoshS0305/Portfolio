Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar browser storage technologies this Site uses, and the choices you have. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser to remember information between visits. Some are essential for a site to function; others are used for analytics or advertising and require your consent in many jurisdictions.

Cookies Used on This Site

Name Provider Purpose Type Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors across sessions Analytics (optional) ~2 years
_ga_<container-id> Google Analytics Persists session state for this property Analytics (optional) ~2 years
cms_authed This site Keeps the site owner signed into the admin CMS panel (not used for visitors) Strictly necessary 30 days

The analytics cookies above (_ga, _ga_*) are only set after you accept the cookie banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, they are never set.

Other Browser Storage (Not Cookies)

This Site also uses localStorage and sessionStorage — browser storage that isn't sent to a server the way cookies are:

  • Content cache — caches this Site's writing/article content in localStorage so pages load faster on repeat visits. Contains no personal information.
  • Admin session state — mirrors the site owner's CMS login state in sessionStorage for the current tab. Not relevant to visitors.
  • Cookie consent choice — your Accept/Decline choice from the banner is itself stored in localStorage so you're not asked again on every visit.

Your Choices

  • The cookie banner — choose Accept or Decline on your first visit; this determines whether analytics cookies are set at all.
  • Change your mind — clear this Site's data in your browser (Site settings → Cookies/Storage) to reset the banner and choose again.
  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies globally; see your browser's help documentation.
  • Google Analytics opt-out — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent GA tracking across all sites.
