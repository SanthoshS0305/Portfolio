Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this personal portfolio website (the "Site"), operated by Santhosh Senthil, collects, uses, and shares information when you visit.

Information I Collect

Automatically, via analytics — only if you accept cookies. If you accept the cookie banner shown on your first visit, this Site uses Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Tag Manager to collect:

  • Your IP address (used to derive an approximate location, then processed by Google)
  • Device, browser, and operating system information
  • Pages viewed, time on site, and referring site or campaign parameters
  • A randomly generated analytics identifier stored in your browser

If you decline or dismiss the cookie banner, none of the above is collected. See the Cookie Policy for full details on the cookies involved.

Information you provide directly. This Site does not have a contact form. If you choose to email me directly, I receive whatever information you include in that email — nothing more.

How I Use This Information

Analytics data is used solely to understand how visitors use the Site (e.g., which pages are popular) so I can improve it. I do not sell data, run advertising, or build marketing lists.

Third-Party Sharing

The only third party that receives visitor data from this Site is Google LLC, as the provider of Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. Google's Data Processing Terms are automatically incorporated into the standard Google Analytics Terms of Service that govern this relationship. You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

No other analytics providers, advertising pixels, or data brokers are used on this Site.

Cookies

This Site uses a small number of cookies and similar browser storage technologies, described in full in the Cookie Policy. Analytics cookies are optional and only load after you accept the cookie banner; a strictly necessary cookie is used solely to keep the Site owner signed into the admin panel and has no effect on visitors.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings for this property. This Site does not maintain its own database of visitor personal information — no visitor data is stored on Site infrastructure.

Your Rights

If you are located in the EU, UK, or another jurisdiction with similar protections, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can withdraw analytics consent immediately by declining the cookie banner or clearing this Site's data in your browser. To exercise any other rights, contact me at the email below.

If you are a California resident, note that this Site's traffic and operations are unlikely to meet the CCPA/CPRA applicability thresholds for a "business" under that law; nonetheless, the rights described above are honored for all visitors regardless of location.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

This Site is served over HTTPS, encrypting data in transit. As noted above, no visitor personal information is stored on Site infrastructure — analytics data is held and secured by Google under its own security commitments.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
